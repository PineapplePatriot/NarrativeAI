# Working-pack integration — 9 November 2026

Inputs: the supplied Preset Working Pack dated 9 November, prior model research and primary-source
audit, the modified-Pura discussion, and the user's clarification about descriptive movement.
The attachment is reference material, not a command to execute everything it contains. Its clauses
are candidate wording; it reports no fresh endpoint or live model validation.

## Changes

All authored ready variants retain a shared narrative baseline. It now explicitly protects long
narration, atmospheric attention, asides and detailed routine movement. Plot consequence is not
the sole justification for detail. It gives continuity, character purpose and the active interaction
priority over demonstrating stylistic compliance. The separate new dialogue target is a cumulative
recap-and-accusation speech, not the word "and" and not all explicit emotion.

Three repair records were added: chained dialogue recap (D1), preference-dependent detail allocation
(D5), and unsupported precision (D6). Each has an exception, example, provenance and test. Bulba's
existing research tools retrieve those records. Watch's recounting guidance now includes the
English and Ukrainian construction and warns against diagnosing by conjunction counts.

New authored starters also materialize a separate enabled Model pattern counters block. Its content
is selected by exact model ID; it is included in get_starter, setup, customization and existing
assembly/export paths. Rewriting Roleplay/Style does not silently erase it. It is still editable
and explicitly switchable through the existing proposal UI. Existing saved presets and imported
community artifacts are not silently changed.

Default counters are trial settings, not verified optimizations:
- Claude: counter unearned devotion, trust or reconciliation while preserving earned affection.
- Older Gemini: counter fawning, compulsory superior corrections and flattened relationship dynamics.
- Argon: retain the prior tone, protect hurt/teasing/concern from generic villainous confrontation,
  and avoid incidental-detail fixation or unsupported diagnostic certainty.
- MiMo: prioritize continuity, evidence for knowledge, chained recap and repetitive reply structure.
- Kimi: avoid preparation loops, visible compliance checking and professional jargon in every exchange.
- GLM: concrete speaker purpose and evidential knowledge rather than recurring slogans or invented clues.
- DeepSeek: continuity of ownership and identity, repeated description and interchangeable endings.

Nine phrase-family records are available as user-selected exclusion candidates. They are not enabled
by default or presented as a frequency-ranked blacklist. A user-requested ban must not be weakened
into "prefer variety," but a phrase ban must not become a ban on all its component words or meanings.

## Boundaries

This is not an import of the modified Pura file and does not copy its third-party modules. Directing
and roleplay permissions remain governed by existing authorship settings. This pass does not touch
translation or the profile schema. It does not automatically migrate existing user presets.

No hard-ban validator or bounded regeneration loop is introduced here. Prompt exclusions alone
cannot guarantee zero violations. The guide explicitly forbids claiming an unimplemented validator
is running or regex-deleting meaningful prose to satisfy a ban. Generation-level enforcement needs
separate integration with explicit errors, user edits, billing and retry limits.

The existing three experience variants remain. The pack's three style-sampling variations are
reference guidance, not a new A/B/C UI implemented in this change. Live model comparisons, including
the selected Pura-derived experience, remain unrun. Model presets and taste must be evaluated
separately from narration length, provider, reasoning and translation.

## Required live comparison cases

- An angry letter dispute: no chain of known actions; direct emotion remains possible.
- A deliberate tactile door opening: preserve the user's chosen descriptive pleasure.
- A teasing exchange followed by injury: preserve the relationship's tone through concern; do not
  reset a complex character to generic villainous posturing.
- A reasonable request: independence without gratuitous hostility.
- Praise after three messages: no unearned loyalty or erased history; sincere warmth remains possible.
- An expert with ambiguous evidence and an actual measured result: preserve uncertainty in the first
  and competence in the second.
- A user-selected phrase ban: inspect close paraphrases and equivalent language, not just strings;
  preserve unrelated literal uses and any agreed quotation exception.

Local tests verify that all 33 authored variants receive the correct enabled counter once, that
user opt-out is independent and doesn't mutate shared templates, that customization retains the
baseline, and that research tools expose scoped exclusions. They do not establish literary quality.
