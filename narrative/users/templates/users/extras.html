Extras

The optional bits around the chat. Change them any time; nothing here is required.

🔊 Voices

Replies can be read aloud through ElevenLabs (a separate account and key): press 🔊 on a reply and the narration is read by a narrator, every speaker in their own voice. Pick voices in a character's Advanced settings (or ask Bulba); anyone without one gets a voice that stays theirs for the chat. Nothing is spent until you press 🔊; a full reply uses about as many ElevenLabs credits as it has characters.

📚 Story summary

Long chats get summarized so the AI remembers older events without rereading everything.

📊 Story trackers

Keeps track of where you are, who's around, relationships, inventory and so on. Which trackers each character uses is picked on that character's Trackers page (from the chat's menu); none are on until you choose.

✉️ Story extras

When the story calls for it, the app draws the object itself: a letter someone finds, the phone screen a character checks, a note when a relationship changes. The AI only supplies the words, so the look never breaks. Not every reply, only when it fits.

🥔 Bulba's ideas

A potato button next to the message box, for when you're stuck. Bulba reads the last few messages and suggests three ways to go on: what you could say or do (pick one to send, or have it written out in your voice), or, when you direct the story, what could happen next. One small call each time you press it.

🎲 Dice and inventory

For game-like stories: the AI asks the app to roll dice when an outcome is uncertain, and the app keeps your inventory and conditions, so nothing is made up. Off means no chance in the story at all. Each character can have its own setting on its Trackers page.

🎭 Character sprites

If a character has pictures for different emotions, the chat shows the one that matches each reply. That takes one small extra call per reply. Pictures can be made with Nano Banana in Gemini.

💸 Background jobs

Summaries, trackers and sprites run alongside your chat with . A cheaper model does them just as well and can save a lot on expensive chat models. It uses the same OpenRouter key.
