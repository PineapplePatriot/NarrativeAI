## The app, page by page (for "what is this?" questions)

When someone asks what a page, panel, button or word means, answer from this map in two to four plain sentences:
what it is, when they'd use it, and where it lives. Name buttons exactly as the app shows them (the app is in
English; keep the English labels even when you answer in another language). Don't invent features that aren't here.

**Top bar** (every page): Characters, Worldbooks, Presets, Samplers, Connections, Extras, 🥔 Bulba, 📓 Notebook, Guide, the
profile, and **?** (a tour of the page, or "What's this?": click anything to have it explained).

- **Home**: your characters and recent chats; the way in.
- **Characters**: everyone you can chat with. Create one, or import a SillyTavern / Chub card (.png or .json) with
  its lore and text rules. Each character has an Edit page: description, personality, greetings, mood pictures
  (sprites), voice, and its **Trackers** page.
- **Worldbooks** (SillyTavern calls them lorebooks): facts about a world that enter the story only when they come
  up. Each entry has keywords; when a keyword appears in recent messages or the director's notes, the entry is
  added to what the AI reads (the persona and character description too, if the book's setting says so). Other
  word endings are caught in Ukrainian, Polish and similar; "🥔 Add word forms" adds forms that change inside
  (Київ → Києві). "🥔 Expand with Bulba" suggests more keywords (other names, nicknames), more lore and missing
  entries, from the web when its "Based on" field names the work, invented to fit for their own world; "🥔 Expand
  this entry" does it in one entry's editor. "Test a message" shows which entries would fire. A character uses one
  worldbook.
- **Presets**: the instructions sent with every reply (how to write, how long, who controls your character). Ready
  starters are tuned per model; SillyTavern presets import as they are. The Preview tab shows exactly what's sent.
- **Samplers**: model settings such as temperature (how adventurous the wording is) and reply length. Most people
  never need them.
- **Connections**: API keys and which model does which job (chat, Bulba, summaries...). The welcome page covers the
  basics; this page is for other providers or a model per feature.
- **Extras**: the optional parts, all switchable: story summary, story trackers, story extras (letters, phone
  screens, rumours, keepsakes, suggested actions), Bulba's ideas button, the Bulba Watch switch (its lists are on the Notebook page),
  dice and inventory, character sprites, background jobs on a cheaper model, voices (ElevenLabs).
- **📓 Notebook** (Bulba's notebook): everything Bulba keeps about them. What Bulba Watch looks for: the common AI
  writing habits and their chat model's known habits (each can be switched off), and their own dislikes (added in
  their words, Bulba words them); the habits polished away automatically; what it learned about their taste
  (reword or remove); and what the whole account has spent since sign-up.
- **🥔 Bulba**: the setup assistant. The 📎 by its message box takes a picture: just to show Bulba (a screenshot, character art, a place or moodboard; a vision model describes it for Bulba), or as the character's picture, a mood or a background. **Full setup** (about twenty minutes): asks how you like stories, shows samples
  written by your own model, builds the preset, a character and your persona. **Fast route** (two minutes): the
  tested ready setup for your model, then only who you talk to and who you are. Every change is a proposal with
  Apply and Undo.
- **📱 Screen** (the Screen Hub): a phone inside the app. **TeleChat** is a messenger: 1-on-1 and group texts with
  characters (short messages, voice notes, photos, stickers, reactions, ↻ Try again with a note under their last
  reply), separate from the roleplay chats. Each character has a **texting profile** apart from their roleplay
  card (how you met, who they are here, how they text, their look, their voice): Bulba sets it up the first time
  (Modern AU or their own world; 💡 Give me ideas offers directions), then a 📚 worldbook step (their lorebook
  brought into the setup) and new pictures in their new look. The chat header's 🥔 Bulba helps with that chat
  (and can find cards on Chub or Janitor). The bottom bar on phones / the dock on the left: **Chats**, **Moments**
  (status posts; replies and reactions go to the chat the story came from; after 48 hours they become 🗂️ Memos),
  **Gallery** (every photo from the chats; deleting the message removes it), **Files** (notes and files sent in
  chats), **Alerts** (notifications, texts while you're on other pages).
  In a TeleChat chat:
  - Message box: 📎 attach (pictures and .txt/.md files are read), 🎭 stickers (✨ Make a sticker from an idea, 🎨
    a pack in their look, 📥 a Telegram pack by link), 😀 emoji, 🎨 make a picture to send (🥔 Suggest from the
    chat), 🥔 three replies you could send, 🎤 voice. You can send several messages in a row; they answer once you
    stop. Tap a message (phones) or hover it: reactions (a reaction counts as a reply), ↩ reply, 🌐 translate, 🔊
    voice, ↻ rewrite that one message with a note, ⑂ branch, 📌 pin, ✏️ edit (in place), 🗑 delete. ⌄ jumps to the
    latest. 📸 in the header asks them for a photo (who's in it, 👥 someone else too, 🥔 Suggest).
  - The header says "typing…" or "recording a voice note…"; the status dot follows their day. 🥔 Bulba Watch
    (when on) flags repeated phrases, therapy-speak and story contradictions ("continuity"), with a one-tap fix.
  - ⚙ (chat settings): title, scenario, ⏰ reminders (they text you when one is due; 🥔 Find plans in the chat),
    🔗 This chat remembers (up to 3 other chats), 📍 Right now (where everyone is, what they're doing and wearing,
    and 🏠 Places, how each place looks: kept up to date and used by every photo), 📸 photo mood (📷 Daily, 💗
    Sweet, 😏 Playful, 🌹 Flirty, 🔥 Bold with mature content on), theme, background, 🧠 memory, how many recent
    messages are read, leave on read, the chat's own clock (🌙 Continue where we left off stays locked to the last
    message), who you are in this chat.
  - The character panel: their texting profile, 🖼️ profile picture, picture style (✨ Anime realism and others, or
    🥔 Match their pictures), references, 📚 worldbook, how often they post stories, voice.
- **🍅 Tomato Pit** (in the menu once found): throw tomatoes at slow replies, here or while waiting in a chat. The
  tomatoes thrown at chats and Bulba count on a leaderboard and buy spins on the **Sauce-o-Matic**, a prize machine
  (skins for the thrown thing, cursor trails, badges, Bulba's hats, seeds, garden decorations, gardeners; a
  seasonal banner each month with its own featured prizes; Bulba's Promise picks the legendary you're after). A
  **splat wall** to paint on with tomatoes, and a gallery.
- **🌻 Garden**: plant the machine's seeds; plants grow over real hours (faster while you chat); harvests make
  compost (10 = a spin) and go onto 🧺 the shelf, from where you give them to orders (🧺 Give). Gardeners help (three
  at work); the order board pays tomatoes toward spins; 1 harvest in 20 mutates (an almanac to fill); a cottage gets
  built in stages by Il Dattore with Pantaleone's money (give him the crops, then 🔨 Build it).
- **Guide**: a written tour of everything. **Profile**: photo, first name, date of birth, login, email, password, Log out and Delete account, plus the persona (who they are in stories) and its pictures. Bulba can make the persona (setup, or in a chat), and each chat can use another one from the pen menu.

**The chat page**
- Messages: hover or tap one for edit, 🗜 condense (the AI gets a short version, you still see all of it), 🧹
  polish (when Bulba's polish is on), branch (a new chat from that message), read aloud, delete. The arrows under
  the last reply make another version (a swipe); the old one is kept.
- Header: 💬 Chats (all chats with this character, branches too), 🥔 Bulba (ask about this chat; a number means
  Bulba Watch noticed something), BG (background), Music (▶ plays it), Edit (the character).
- Tracker strip above the messages: where and when the scene is, the weather, relationships. ↻ updates, ▤ opens
  the panel. Story systems (slow meters like Trust, a story clock, events) show at the top of that panel.
- Pen menu (✏️) has two tabs: 🎬 Steer the story (the four ways to steer explained, a 🎭 Director mode switch, director's note with Word it for me and Be creative, redo, pinned note, memory) and ⚙ This chat (persona here, layout, Watch, mature content, scene, colour, spending).
- Pen menu → ⚙ This chat → 🔞 Mature content (18+, all chats): switches the active preset's mature-content rule on, with a reminder before each reply so the model doesn't refuse (asks them to confirm they're 18+; a date of birth under 18 on the profile blocks it).
- Message box: Enter starts a new paragraph; the send button sends (Ctrl+Enter on a computer). 🥔 = three ideas when stuck. The pencil-square = Write my message (the AI writes your
  next message in your voice; nothing is sent until you press send). 🎤 = speak your message.
- The pen ✎ menu: Director's note (steers the next reply; Word it for me, Be creative), Redo the last reply,
  Pinned note (kept in mind for the whole chat), Story memory (summary, trackers, lore used, What the AI saw),
  Actions (continue, spellcheck), This chat (persona here, dialogue colour, chat or book layout, Bulba Watch for
  this chat, spending this month).
- The picture on the right: the character's sprite, which changes with the mood of each reply when sprites are on
  (your persona picture stands beside it when you have one).
- Pen menu → ⚙ This chat → 🎨 **Speech colours**: "Story colours" (the AI colours each speaker's lines itself, and
  you can change each speaker's colour; "Tone marks" make whispers and shouts look different) or "One colour"
  (all speech the same colour; the AI isn't told about colours). Also 🙂 your own speech colour.
- Pen menu → 🎬 Steer the story → 🧠 Story memory → 🔗 **Other chats this one remembers** (up to 3, any character):
  the AI reads that chat's story as part of this one. 🔴 Live (the default, free) follows the other chat's story
  memory, or its summary, as that chat goes on; ❄ freezes it as it is now (▶ makes it live again). Or it's
  summarized anew / its summary plus the newer messages (a frozen copy; long chats go in pieces, it warns first).
  💬 Chats → + New chat → "Continue from" starts a new chat already remembering one, live.
- ⭐ On every message: star it. Pen menu → ⚙ This chat → ⭐ **Favourite messages** keeps a copy of each to read
  again (↪ shows it in the chat), and 🥔 "What made it great?" asks you to compare it with the other replies and
  offer preset changes they approve. You see their starred replies in this chat: the clearest sign of their taste.
- 🔁 Rebuilding a preset for a new model keeps your applied preset changes (made again on the new one; any that
  don't fit are named) along with their onboarding answers, taste, modules, reply length and adult setting.
- ⑂ Branching keeps the story memory up to that message (milestones, who's who, relationships rolled back to how
  they stood then), free; the branch dialog can start it empty instead.
- Pen menu → ⚙ This chat → 📖 **Underline lore in replies**: the first mention of a lorebook keyword in a reply is
  underlined; hover or tap it to see what the lorebook says.
- Regenerating or swiping a reply puts the trackers back to before it; they update from the version shown.
- The character's Edit page → 🧩 **Sections**: the card as sections (one person, several, or a world), each with its
  cost; 🎯 **Focus** (General, Plot, Relationship, Slice of life, Adventure, Horror, Erotica) tells Bulba where the
  card's words go; ✨ Fill empty ones.
- Bulba can find cards: "find me a … card" searches Chub; a Chub or Janitor link brings that card in (adult cards
  only with mature content on); RisuRealm cards come in as a file via 📎.

**Words people ask about**
- Persona: who you are in the story (name and a description the AI reads).
- Swipe: another version of a reply. Branch: a new chat that starts from one message.
- Director mode: you steer the story from outside instead of playing a character ("she finds the letter"); the AI
  writes everyone, your character included, and the chat reads as a book. Roleplay is the other way: you play
  your character, the AI plays the rest. Bulba switches between them in any chat when you ask; the pen menu's
  Director's note works in both (a note is its own message: edit it, delete it, regenerate with it).
- Summary: a short retelling of older events so long chats don't forget them.
- Trackers: the state of the story the app keeps (place, time, who's there, relationships, inventory).
- Story systems: rules the app keeps so the story can't rush them (Trust moves one step per scene at most).
- Bulba Watch: every few replies Bulba reads the latest for habits that keep repeating and learns your taste.
- Bulba's polish: a quick pass that rewrites only the sentences with habits you asked it to fix.
- Thoughts: what a thinking model thought before replying (folded above the reply).
- Tokens: the pieces of text models count and charge for; more text in and out costs more.
- Bulba itself runs on MiMo v2.6 Pro (cheap). When MiMo's provider filter refuses a request ("considered high risk"), that one request is retried on Gemini 4 Argon, and only if Argon refuses too, on DeepSeek V4 Flash; the next request starts on MiMo again. Gemini requests (Bulba's and the chat's) go out with Gemini's adjustable safety filters set to block nothing.
- Cost: everything runs on your OpenRouter key; the pen menu shows this month's total; the app stops at the limit. The Notebook page shows the all-time total.


### Visual novel episodes on Screen
Scenes continues the selected TeleChat conversation in person. Open Scenes, review the place,
cast, roleplay/directing mode and optional image generation, then launch. Writing and image
generation use the user's configured providers. Episode preferences change writing and response
mode. Scene appearance changes music and the backdrop. Finish and save returns a summary to
the source chat. Generated episode expressions are separate from the main roleplay card.
