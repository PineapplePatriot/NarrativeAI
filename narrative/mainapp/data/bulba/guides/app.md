## The app, page by page (for "what is this?" questions)

When someone asks what a page, panel, button or word means, answer from this map in two to four plain sentences:
what it is, when they'd use it, and where it lives. Name buttons exactly as the app shows them (the app is in
English; keep the English labels even when you answer in another language). Don't invent features that aren't here.

**Top bar** (every page): Characters, Worldbooks, Presets, Samplers, Connections, Extras, 🥔 Bulba, Guide, the
profile, and **?** (a tour of the page, or "What's this?": click anything to have it explained).

- **Home**: your characters and recent chats; the way in.
- **Characters**: everyone you can chat with. Create one, or import a SillyTavern / Chub card (.png or .json) with
  its lore and text rules. Each character has an Edit page: description, personality, greetings, mood pictures
  (sprites), voice, and its **Trackers** page.
- **Worldbooks** (SillyTavern calls them lorebooks): facts about a world that enter the story only when they come
  up. Each entry has keywords; when a keyword appears in recent messages, the entry is added to what the AI reads.
  "Test a message" shows which entries would fire. A character uses one worldbook.
- **Presets**: the instructions sent with every reply (how to write, how long, who controls your character). Ready
  starters are tuned per model; SillyTavern presets import as they are. The Preview tab shows exactly what's sent.
- **Samplers**: model settings such as temperature (how adventurous the wording is) and reply length. Most people
  never need them.
- **Connections**: API keys and which model does which job (chat, Bulba, summaries...). The welcome page covers the
  basics; this page is for other providers or a model per feature.
- **Extras**: the optional parts, all switchable: story summary, story trackers, story extras (letters, phone
  screens, rumours, keepsakes, suggested actions), Bulba's ideas button, Bulba Watch (and the habits it fixes),
  dice and inventory, character sprites, background jobs on a cheaper model, voices (ElevenLabs).
- **🥔 Bulba**: the setup assistant. **Full setup** (about twenty minutes): asks how you like stories, shows samples
  written by your own model, builds the preset, your persona and a character. **Fast route** (two minutes): the
  tested ready setup for your model, then only who you are and who you talk to. Every change is a proposal with
  Apply and Undo.
- **Guide**: a written tour of everything. **Profile**: photo, persona (who you are in stories), password.

**The chat page**
- Messages: hover or tap one for edit, 🗜 condense (the AI gets a short version, you still see all of it), 🧹
  polish (when Bulba's polish is on), branch (a new chat from that message), read aloud, delete. The arrows under
  the last reply make another version (a swipe); the old one is kept.
- Header: 💬 Chats (all chats with this character, branches too), 🥔 Bulba (ask about this chat; a number means
  Bulba Watch noticed something), BG (background), Music (▶ plays it), Edit (the character).
- Tracker strip above the messages: where and when the scene is, the weather, relationships. ↻ updates, ▤ opens
  the panel. Story systems (slow meters like Trust, a story clock, events) show at the top of that panel.
- Message box: Enter sends. 🥔 = three ideas when stuck. The pencil-square = Write my message (the AI writes your
  next message in your voice; nothing is sent until you press send). 🎤 = speak your message.
- The pen ✎ menu: Director's note (steers the next reply; Word it for me, Be creative), Redo the last reply,
  Pinned note (kept in mind for the whole chat), Story memory (summary, trackers, lore used, What the AI saw),
  Actions (continue, spellcheck), This chat (persona here, dialogue colour, chat or book layout, Bulba Watch for
  this chat, spending this month).
- The picture on the right: the character's sprite, which changes with the mood of each reply when sprites are on.

**Words people ask about**
- Persona: who you are in the story (name and a description the AI reads).
- Swipe: another version of a reply. Branch: a new chat that starts from one message.
- Director mode: you steer the story from outside instead of playing a character ("she finds the letter"); the AI
  writes everyone, your character included, and the chat reads as a book. Roleplay is the other way: you play
  your character, the AI plays the rest. Bulba switches between them in any chat when you ask; the pen menu's
  Director's note works in both (a note is its own message: edit it, delete it, regenerate with it).
- Summary: a short retelling of older events so long chats don't forget them.
- Trackers: the state of the story the app keeps (place, time, who's there, relationships, inventory).
- Story systems: rules the app keeps so the story can't rush them (Trust moves one step per scene at most).
- Bulba Watch: every few replies Bulba reads the latest for habits that keep repeating and learns your taste.
- Bulba's polish: a quick pass that rewrites only the sentences with habits you asked it to fix.
- Thoughts: what a thinking model thought before replying (folded above the reply).
- Tokens: the pieces of text models count and charge for; more text in and out costs more.
- Cost: everything runs on your OpenRouter key; the pen menu shows this month's total; the app stops at the limit.
