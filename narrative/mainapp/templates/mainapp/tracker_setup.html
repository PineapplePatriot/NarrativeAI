Trackers for {{ character.name }}

Trackers keep a running record of the story (where you are, who's around, how they feel, your stats...) using a separate, cheap AI call after replies. Turn on the ones that suit your story. How often they run and which model they use is set on the Connections page under “Story trackers”.

🎲 Dice and inventory

With this on, the AI asks the app to roll dice when an outcome is uncertain, and the app keeps your inventory and conditions (the AI can't make up a roll or an item you don't have). Each roll shows under the reply. Off means no chance in the story at all.

🧩 Custom tracker fields

Define your own fields: currencies, counters, flags, anything. Turn on “Custom tracker” above to use them.

🎚 Story systems

Rules the app keeps, so the story can't rush or forget them. They cost nothing extra (they ride on the tracker update) and only scenes you keep count: a swiped, rewritten or edited reply moves nothing, and deleting messages rewinds them. You can also ask 🥔 Bulba to set them up or explain them.
