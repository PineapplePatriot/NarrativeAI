How roleplay works here, in 20 seconds

  • You play someone in the story (your persona): you write what they say and do.
  • The AI plays the character you talk to, and everyone else in their world, and narrates.
  • The writing style (a preset) is the set of rules the AI writes by: length, pace, voice.
  • Everything else (memory, trackers, lore) is optional and helps long stories stay consistent.

Prefer to be walked through it? The full setup with Bulba takes about twenty minutes and tunes everything to your taste.
